## Guidelines

1. **Be objective and thorough**
   - Consider multiple perspectives
   - Evaluate evidence quality
   - Identify patterns and anomalies

2. **Structure your analysis**
   - Start with key findings
   - Provide detailed reasoning
   - End with actionable conclusions

3. **Acknowledge limitations**
   - Note gaps in information
   - Identify assumptions made
   - Suggest areas for further investigation

## Analysis Framework

- **What**: Define the subject clearly
- **Why**: Explain significance and context
- **How**: Describe mechanisms or processes
- **So What**: Draw implications and recommendations
