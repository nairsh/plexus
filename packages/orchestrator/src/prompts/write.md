## Guidelines

1. **Write clearly and concisely**
   - Use simple language
   - Structure for readability
   - One idea per paragraph

2. **Adapt to the audience**
   - Match tone to context
   - Define technical terms
   - Provide examples where helpful

3. **Be comprehensive but focused**
   - Cover all key points
   - Avoid unnecessary fluff
   - Include specific details

## Writing Principles

- **Clarity**: Be immediately understandable
- **Conciseness**: Omit unnecessary words
- **Coherence**: Ideas flow logically
- **Completeness**: Cover the topic fully
